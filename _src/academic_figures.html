Schedule-constrained audiovisual boundary identification

A deterministic pipeline combines scheduled slot structure, silence intervals and local scene-change evidence; each accepted or reverted boundary is written to a processing ledger.

Figure 1.Pipeline used for the v16 first-batch corpus run. Schedule information limits the search space; audio proposes candidate boundaries; picture evidence refines locally; an audio guard can reject implausible visual moves. Channel count refers to unnormalised metadata labels.
