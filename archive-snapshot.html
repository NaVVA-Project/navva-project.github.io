A national broadcast record

Britain’s advertising archive, at broadcast scale.

Since January 2016, the BFI’s metadata supply records 18.44 million advert broadcasts representing 136,929 unique adverts—an average of 134.7 screenings per advert. 134.7 is an arithmetic mean across the full metadata supply. Without a distribution or median, it should not be read as a typical advert.

01 · Programme record

A collection built in two eras.

The advert metadata sits alongside more than one million preserved television programmes containing advertising. The programme counts are a separate measure from individual advert broadcasts.

03 · Method note

From archive to research corpus.

These figures describe the metadata supply and the preserved programme collection. They are not model outputs and were not produced by boundary detection.

Continuous broadcast recordings still require identification, segmentation, validation and provenance tracking before they become a research corpus of individual advert clips.
