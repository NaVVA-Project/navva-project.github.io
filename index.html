From Narratives of Value to Valuable Algorithms

A decade of promotional screen culture in the UK, seen by machine

01 · The archive

Ten years of television advertising in the BFI National Archive

The BFI National Archive has recorded television off air from UK public service broadcasters since the 1980s. Since 2016 these recordings include the advertisements broadcast on the main commercial public service channels. Taken together, the adverts are likely to be one of the largest collections of their kind, and a record of consumer culture in the UK over the past decade.

The adverts are spread across thousands of hours of digital video and have not yet been documented as a collection. NaVVA sets out to define this material as a usable collection, to study it as a record of social and economic values in the UK, and to produce documentation that opens it to researchers in other disciplines and to wider audiences.

02 · The questions

Two questions about a decade of promotional screen culture

How has promotional screen culture in Britain changed over the past decade?

How can this change be accessed, understood and analysed from a computational perspective?

Within this frame, the thesis looks at how British national identity is presented in television advertising between 2016 and 2026.

03 · The approach

Computational methods combined with close reading

Archive

Building the collection

Single advertisements are located in the continuous recordings and extracted, so that each advert becomes an item that can be described, counted and compared.

Machine

Viewing at scale

A vision-language model is used to describe what the adverts show across the whole collection, so that patterns over the decade can be mapped.

Human

Reading closely

A smaller selection of adverts is analysed in detail with approaches from media studies and social semiotics, and the two kinds of reading are compared.

05 · Status

In progress, 2025 to 2029

The project began in October 2025. Work so far has concentrated on corpus construction, that is, on cutting single adverts out of the recordings and measuring how accurate the cuts are. Further code, documentation and publications will be linked from this page as they are released.

advert-silence-boundary is the code and method documentation for the corpus construction step. It cuts single adverts out of broadcast recordings from silences in the soundtrack, the broadcast schedule and scene changes in the picture.
